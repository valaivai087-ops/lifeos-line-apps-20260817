Q1–2 Read Aloud 朗讀
Speaking ⏱ 45秒準備 → 45秒朗讀 評分重點：發音・語調・流暢度
1
發音 Pronunciation
每個音節清楚發出，不吃字、不省略
特別注意：-ed /t/ /d/ /ɪd/・-s /s/ /z/ /ɪz/・-ing・-tion /ʃən/
2
重音 Stress
單字重音 + 句子情感重音（強調關鍵字）
例：The MEETING starts at NINE. / Please REGISTER by Friday.
3
停頓 Pause
逗號 → 短停（1拍）・句號 → 長停（2拍）・換行自然換氣
不要一口氣讀完整段，停頓讓評分員聽清楚每個句子
4
語調 Intonation
陳述句 ↘ 下降・Yes/No問句 ↗ 上升・列舉最後一項 ↘ 下降
單調平讀扣分，語調起伏顯示理解力
⭐ 45秒準備策略：① 快速瀏覽全段標出難字 ② 在逗號、句號做停頓記號 ③ 圈出長單字確認重音位置 ④ 朗讀速度放慢 20%，評分員重視清晰度 > 速度
-ed 結尾發音
voiced→/d/・unvoiced→/t/・t,d結尾→/ɪd/
-tion / -sion
→ /ʃən/・conference, presentation
-ture
→ /tʃər/・feature, future, lecture
連音 Linking
pick_it_up / turn_it_on / look_at_it
✏ 練習文本：朗讀以下段落（計時45秒）
The annual sales conference will be held on November 15th,
at the Grand Hotel in downtown Chicago.
All regional managers are expected to attend
and should register online by October 31st.
The registration fee is fifty dollars per person,
which includes lunch and all conference materials.
朗讀重點
• November 15th → NOvember / fif-TEENTH（重音在後） • downtown Chicago → DOWN-town Chi-CA-go • expected to attend → ex-PEC-ted / at-TEND • 逗號後停頓：...Chicago, ↓ [停] All regional... • fifty dollars → FIF-ty DOL-lars（兩個重音） • 最後一句語調 ↘ 下降，表示結束
✔ 自我檢核
Q3 Describe a Picture 照片描述
Speaking ⏱ 30秒準備 → 45秒描述 6段結構 · 絕對不能只說1-2句
1
開頭 Setting
This picture was taken in / at [地點].
先說地點，建立整體場景 → 1句
2
前景 Foreground
In the foreground, I can see [主體 + 動作].
最近、最明顯的人或物 → 2句
3
動作 Action
The person is [V-ing]. / They appear to be [V-ing].
現在進行式，用 appear to be 表示推測 → 1-2句
4
中景 Middle
In the middle of the picture, there is/are [物品/人群].
中間區域的人或物 → 1-2句
5
背景 Background
In the background, I can see [環境細節].
遠處、場景環境 → 1句
6
結尾 Overall
Overall, it looks like [情境推測] is taking place.
總結氣氛或推測場景，給人完整感 → 1句
萬用模板
This picture was taken in a/an [地點]. In the foreground, I can see [主詞]. The [person] appears to be [V-ing]. In the middle of the picture, there is/are [物/人]. In the background, I can see [背景細節]. Overall, it looks like [情境] is taking place.
⭐ 必備動詞庫（現在進行式）：
sitting, standing, walking, talking, working, wearing, holding, looking at, pointing at, carrying, using, writing, reading, smiling, discussing, presenting, typing
辦公室場景
at a desk / in an office / in a conference room / at a computer
戶外場景
in a park / on a street / near a building / at a station
餐廳/商店
at a counter / in a restaurant / at a cash register / in a store
推測句型
appear to be / seem to be / look like they are / might be
✏ 練習：用6段結構描述（辦公室：2人開會，桌上有筆電和文件）
範例答案
This picture was taken in a conference room. In the foreground, I can see two people sitting across from each other at a large table. They appear to be having a business meeting. In the middle of the picture, there are a laptop, some documents, and two cups of coffee on the table. In the background, I can see a whiteboard with some writing on it and large windows with natural light coming in. Overall, it looks like an important business discussion is taking place.
這張照片拍攝於會議室。前景是兩人坐在大桌子兩側，似乎正在進行商務會議。桌子中間有筆記型電腦、一些文件和兩杯咖啡。背景是一面有字跡的白板，以及透著自然光的大窗戶。整體看來像是一場重要的商務討論正在進行。

Q4–6 Respond to Questions 回答問題
Speaking ⏱ Q4-5 各15秒 · Q6 30秒 直接回答 · 不要猶豫
A
Answer 直接回答
Yes, I do. / No, I don't. / I think... / I prefer...
第一句就給答案，不要說 "Hmm, well, I think..."
R
Reason 原因
Because... / The reason is that... / This is because...
給一個具體理由（Q4-5 到這裡就足夠）
E
Example 例子
For example, ... / For instance, ... / Specifically, ...
舉具體例子，讓答案有說服力（Q6必用）
C
Conclusion 結論
That's why... / So... / This is why I prefer...
Q6專用收尾，呼應開頭答案
Q4–5 模板（15秒）
A Yes, I think [answer]. / I prefer [answer]. R Because [reason].
Q6 模板（30秒 · 完整四段）
A I prefer [answer]. R Because [reason]. E For example, [specific example]. C That's why I [restate answer].
⭐ 時間控制：Q4-5 只有15秒 → Answer + Reason 兩段即可，不要硬擠例子。Q6 有30秒 → 四段都要有，每段約7-8秒。
✏ Q6 練習題：Do you prefer working from home or in an office? Give reasons.
範例答案（Q6 · 30秒）
I prefer working in the office. Because I can communicate with my colleagues more easily. For example, when I have a question, I can ask someone right away instead of waiting for an email reply. That's why I prefer going to the office every day.
我偏好在辦公室工作。因為可以更容易與同事溝通。例如有問題時可以立刻詢問，不需要等待郵件回覆。這就是為什麼我每天都偏好去辦公室。
✔ 自我檢核
Q7–9 Respond Using Information 看資料回答
Speaking ⏱ 30秒閱讀 · Q7-8 各15秒 · Q9 30秒 直接讀資料 · 不需個人意見
1
引用資料開頭
According to the schedule / information / announcement, ...
第一句一定要引用來源，不能直接說答案
2
說明資料內容
The schedule shows that... / It says that... / According to the email, ...
忠實轉述資料，不加個人判斷
3
時間格式
It starts at 9 a.m. and ends at 5 p.m. / from 9 to 5
時間一定用 at・數字用英文念出
4
地點格式
It will be held at / in [地點名稱].
地點用 at 接機構名，in 接城市/國家
5
費用格式
The fee / cost / price is [金額]. / It costs [金額].
直接讀出數字，$50 → fifty dollars
6
資料沒有時
I'm sorry, but that information is not given / provided.
資料中沒有的資訊，不能猜，說這句就好
萬用模板
According to the [schedule/email/flyer], the [event] will be held on [date]. It starts at [time] and ends at [time]. It will take place at [location]. The registration fee is [amount]. I'm sorry, but that information is not given.
⭐ Q9 通常是最難的：常考「多個項目比較」或「日程中不存在的資訊」。看到問題後，先找關鍵字在資料中的位置，再用模板轉述。不確定時直接說 "I'm sorry, that information is not given."
✏ 練習資料：Workshop on Digital Marketing
Date: March 20 | Time: 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM | Venue: Grand Hotel | Fee: $80
Q7: When is the workshop?
Q8: Where will it be held?
Q9: Is lunch included in the fee?（資料中未提及）
範例答案
Q7: According to the schedule, the workshop will be held on March 20th. It starts at nine a.m. and ends at four p.m. Q8: According to the information, it will take place at the Grand Hotel. Q9: I'm sorry, but that information is not given in the schedule.
✔ 自我檢核
Q10 Propose a Solution 提供解決方案
Speaking ⏱ 30秒準備 → 60秒回答 5段結構 · 電話留言語氣
1
Problem 確認問題
I understand that you have a concern about [問題].
先說明自己理解對方的困境，表示同理
2
Apology 道歉
I sincerely apologize for the inconvenience (caused).
真誠道歉，不找藉口，不說理由
3
Solution 1 第一方案
I suggest that we [具體方案].
說清楚，加上對方的好處
4
Solution 2 備選方案
Another option is to [備選方案].
必須給兩個選項，讓對方有選擇空間
5
Closing 收尾
I hope this helps. Please feel free to contact us if you need anything else.
表示願意進一步協助，禮貌結束
萬用電話腳本模板
Hello, this is [name] calling from [company]. I understand that you have a concern about [problem]. I sincerely apologize for the inconvenience. I suggest that we [Solution 1]. Another option is to [Solution 2]. I hope this helps. Please feel free to contact us if you need anything else. Thank you and have a great day.
⭐ 必備萬用句：
道歉：I sincerely apologize for any inconvenience this may have caused.
方案1：I would like to offer you a complimentary upgrade / full refund / rescheduled appointment.
方案2：Alternatively, we can arrange / provide / send you...
收尾：I assure you that we will do our best to resolve this issue.
✏ 練習情境：客戶訂的飯店房間已滿，無法入住，請致電道歉並提供兩個解決方案。
範例答案（60秒）
Hello, this is Sarah calling from Grand View Hotel. I understand that you have a concern about your room reservation for this weekend. I sincerely apologize for the inconvenience this has caused. I suggest that we upgrade your room to our deluxe suite at absolutely no extra charge. Another option is to arrange accommodation for you at our partner hotel nearby, and we will cover the transportation cost. I hope this helps resolve the issue. Please feel free to call us anytime, and we will do our best to make sure your stay is comfortable. Thank you and have a great day.
您好，我是Grand View Hotel的Sarah。我了解您對本週末房間訂位有疑慮，對於造成的不便我深感抱歉。建議我們免費為您升級到豪華套房，另一個選擇是安排您入住附近的合作飯店，我們將承擔交通費用。希望這能解決問題，請隨時聯絡我們。

Q11 Express an Opinion 表達意見
Speaking ⏱ 15秒準備 → 60秒回答 6段結構 · 兩個理由必須有
1
Opinion 立場
I think / I believe / In my opinion, [立場]. There are two main reasons.
開門見山，不猶豫。預告「兩個理由」讓結構清晰
2
Reason 1 理由一
First, / To begin with, [理由1].
具體且直接的原因，不要太抽象
3
Example 1 例子一
For example, / For instance, [具體例子].
用「具體數字、人、情境」支撐理由
4
Reason 2 理由二
Second, / In addition, / Furthermore, [理由2].
不同角度的第二個理由
5
Example 2 例子二
For instance, / This means that [例子2].
第二個具體例子，不能和第一個重複
6
Conclusion 結論
Therefore, / For these reasons, I believe [重申立場].
呼應開頭，重申立場，給人完整感
萬用模板（60秒 = 每段約10秒）
I think [立場/意見]. There are two main reasons. First, [Reason 1]. For example, [Example 1]. Second, [Reason 2]. For instance, [Example 2]. Therefore, I believe [重申立場].
⭐ 萬用立場句（任何題目都能用）：
同意：I strongly believe that... / I think the advantages outweigh the disadvantages.
不同意：I don't think... / In my opinion, this is not a good idea because...
萬用理由庫：saves time / saves money / improves productivity / reduces stress / helps communication / is more efficient / is more convenient / is better for health
✏ 練習題：Do you think companies should allow employees to work from home?
範例答案（60秒）
I think companies should allow employees to work from home. There are two main reasons. First, working from home improves productivity. For example, employees can avoid long commutes and use that extra time to focus on their work. Studies show that remote workers are often more productive than office workers. Second, it reduces costs for both companies and employees. For instance, businesses can save on office space and utilities, while workers spend less on transportation and meals. Therefore, I believe that allowing remote work is beneficial for everyone involved.
我認為公司應允許員工在家工作，主要有兩個理由。首先，在家工作提升生產力，員工可以省去通勤時間並專注工作。其次，可以降低公司和員工的成本，企業節省辦公空間，員工節省交通和餐飲費用。因此，我相信允許遠距工作對所有人都有益。
✔ 自我檢核
W Q1–5 Sentence Building 看圖造句
Writing 每題8分鐘 · 一句話包含兩個關鍵字
主詞 Subject
+
動詞 is/are V-ing
+
地點 Location
+
（細節 Detail）
1
主詞 Subject
The man / The woman / A group of people / Two workers / Several customers
根據圖中人物選擇，注意單複數
2
動詞 Verb（現在進行式）
is sitting / is working / is talking / are walking / are wearing / are carrying
一律用 is/are + V-ing，不用其他時態
3
地點 Location
in the office / at the station / near the building / on the street / at a desk
in = 封閉空間内・at = 地點旁邊・on = 表面上
4
細節 Detail（加分）
while holding a pen / with a colleague / and smiling / wearing a uniform
加 while / with / and 連接細節，句子更豐富
句型模板
The [man/woman] is [V-ing] in/at [location]. The [person] is [V-ing] [object] while [V-ing]. Several people are [V-ing] near [location]. A woman appears to be [V-ing] at [location].
辦公室動作
typing, presenting, filing, photocopying, answering a call, reviewing documents
公共場所動作
waiting, checking in, paying, browsing, lining up, crossing the street
工地/商店動作
stacking, loading, arranging, inspecting, demonstrating, installing
地點介系詞
in the office / at a desk / on the floor / near the entrance / beside the shelf
⭐ 題目給兩個關鍵字：兩個字都必須出現在句子中，且文法正確。例如：關鍵字 woman / laptop → The woman is using a laptop in the office while talking on the phone.
✏ 練習：用以下關鍵字各造一句（woman・laptop）（man・phone）（people・meeting）
範例答案
1. The woman is using a laptop at her desk while taking notes in a notebook. 2. The man is talking on the phone near the entrance of the office building. 3. Several people are having a meeting in the conference room and looking at a presentation on the screen.

W Q6–7 Respond to an Email 回覆 Email
Writing ⏱ Q6 各10分鐘 · Q7 各10分鐘 Q6 約75字 · Q7 約100字
1
Greeting 稱呼
Dear [Name], / Dear Sir or Madam,
用 Dear，不要用 Hello 或 Hi
2
Purpose 目的
Thank you for your email. I am writing in response to your questions.
開頭感謝 + 說明回信目的，2句話
3
Answer 回答
In answer to your first question, ... / Regarding your question about [topic], ...
每個問題都要回答，不能漏掉
4
Details 細節
First, ... / Second, ... / Please note that ... / Additionally, ...
用 First/Second 分段，清楚有條理
5
Closing 結尾
If you need any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me.
表示願意進一步協助
6
Sign-off 署名
Best regards, / Sincerely, / Kind regards,
空行後署名，必須有（否則扣分）
萬用 Email 模板
Dear [Name],   Thank you for your email regarding [topic]. I am writing to answer your questions.   First, [answer to Q1].   Second, [answer to Q2].   If you need any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me.   Best regards, [Your name]
⭐ 高分關鍵：① 每個問題都回答（Q6至少回答1題，Q7至少2題）② 不要複製題目的原句，用自己的話改寫 ③ 加入細節：日期、時間、金額、條件 ④ 結尾 If you need... 是固定句，一定要有
✏ 練習：對方問了 (1)研討會日期 (2)費用包含什麼。請回覆（日期：3月15日・費用$80含午餐）
範例答案
Dear Ms. Johnson, Thank you for your email regarding our upcoming seminar. I am writing to answer your questions. First, the seminar will be held on March 15th at the Business Center on Fifth Avenue. Second, the registration fee is $80 per person, which includes a buffet lunch and all seminar materials. If you need any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me. Best regards, David Chen
親愛的Johnson女士，感謝您詢問即將舉行的研討會。首先，研討會將於3月15日在第五大道商業中心舉行。其次，報名費為每人80美元，包含自助午餐及所有研討會資料。如需更多資訊，請隨時聯繫。

W Q8 Opinion Essay 意見作文
Writing ⏱ 30分鐘 · 目標 200字 結構比創意重要 · 評分員看段落
1
Introduction 引言
I agree/disagree that [topic]. There are several reasons.
立場清楚 + 預告理由，2句即可
2
Reason 1 理由一
First, [reason 1].
第一段：一個清楚的理由
3
Example 1 例子一
For example, [specific example].
具體例子 2-3句，越具體越好
4
Reason 2 理由二
Second, [reason 2].
第二段：不同角度的理由
5
Example 2 例子二
For instance, [example 2].
第二個具體例子 2-3句
6
Conclusion 結論
Therefore, I believe [restate position].
呼應開頭，重申立場，1-2句
萬用作文模板
I agree that [topic/statement]. There are several reasons. First, [Reason 1]. For example, [Example 1]. [Add 1-2 more sentences to expand]. Second, [Reason 2]. For instance, [Example 2]. [Add 1-2 more sentences to expand]. Therefore, I believe [restate opinion].
⭐ 萬用理由庫（任何題目都能套用）：
saves time and money・improves productivity・reduces stress・promotes teamwork・is more convenient・helps people develop skills・is better for the environment・increases job satisfaction・leads to better results・allows more flexibility
連接詞（前進）
Furthermore, / In addition, / Moreover, / Also,
連接詞（結論）
Therefore, / As a result, / For these reasons, / In conclusion,
例子引導
For example, / For instance, / Such as / A good example is
立場句型
I agree that / I believe / In my opinion / I strongly feel that
✏ 練習題：Do you agree that companies should provide free meals for employees?
範例答案（約200字）
I agree that companies should provide free meals for employees. There are several reasons. First, free meals save employees time and money. For example, workers do not need to spend time looking for lunch options or waiting in long restaurant queues. This extra time can be used to rest or prepare for afternoon meetings, which leads to higher productivity after lunch. Second, sharing meals in the workplace encourages teamwork and better communication. For instance, when employees eat together in a company cafeteria, they have the opportunity to interact with colleagues from different departments. This informal communication can lead to new ideas and stronger working relationships. Therefore, I believe that providing free meals is a worthwhile investment for any company that wants to improve employee satisfaction and overall performance.
我同意公司應提供員工免費餐點。首先，免費餐點節省員工時間和金錢，員工不需要花時間尋找午餐或排隊，省下的時間可以休息或準備下午的會議，提升下午生產力。其次，共用餐廳促進團隊合作和溝通，員工可以與不同部門同事互動，激發新想法並建立更好的工作關係。因此，我相信提供免費餐點是提升員工滿意度和整體績效的值得投資。
