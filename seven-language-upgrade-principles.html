【核心母命題】語言的七個功能
語言同時做這七件事
▼
中文
語言不是只用來描述世界。

語言同時在切割世界（分類）、解釋世界（因果）、預測世界（未來）、賦予權重（優先序）、生成情緒（感受）、限制選項（可能性）、驅動行動（行為）。

所以改變語言，不只是「換個說法」。

而是在修改大腦處理現實的模型。

真正高維的語言，不急著評價人。

而是把問題重新轉譯成「可以觀察、可以拆解、可以驗證、可以更新、可以行動」的結構。
English
Language doesn't just describe.

Language simultaneously carves (categorizes), interprets (causation), predicts (futures), assigns weights (priorities), generates emotions (sensations), constrains options (possibilities), and drives action (behaviors).

Changing language is not simply rewording.

It modifies how your brain processes reality.

True high-dimensional language doesn't rush to judge.

It retranslates problems into observable, decomposable, verifiable, updatable, and actionable structures.

English
01 Events are data awaiting analysis, not verdicts.

02 Emotions are signals, not enemies.

03 Errors are model-reality mismatches, not shame.

04 Limits are buildable capabilities, not identity.

05 Success is extractable patterns, not endpoints.

06 Failure is training data for your next version.

07 Each day is an opportunity to update your life model.
