第1題：搭配陷阱 ★★★ 必考
下列何者正確？
(A) economic car
(B) economical car
(C) efficient car
(D) effective car
✓ 答案：B
economical car = 省油車 = 節省燃油資源
🚫 陷阱分析：
A (economic car) = 經濟系統的車 (無意義)
這是同詞族陷阱，最常考！
第2題：文意克漏字 ★★★ 必考
The company adopted _____ methods to reduce operational costs .
(A) economic
(B) economical
(C) efficient
(D) effective
✓ 答案：B (economical)
關鍵詞：「reduce costs」= 降低成本
→ 需要「節省資源」的方法
→ economical methods (節省成本的方法)
🚫 常選錯：C (efficient)
efficient = 工作流程順暢/高效率
但文意強調「降低成本」，非「提高效率」
第3題：近義詞區分 ★★ 常考
The government promoted the _____ use of water to address water shortages .
(A) economical
(B) efficient
(C) productive
(D) effective
✓ 答案：A (economical)
文意分析：「address water shortages」= 解決水資源短缺
→ 需要節約用水
→ economical use of water = 節儉地用水
🚫 常選錯：B (efficient)
efficient use of water = 高效率地用水
但「解決水短缺」需要「節省」，不是「高效」
第4題：詞族混淆 ★★ 常考
Which sentence is most natural ?
(A) The economic choice is to buy a cheaper car.
(B) The economical choice is to buy a cheaper car.
(C) Both are equally correct .
(D) Neither is correct.
✓ 答案：B (economical choice)
economical choice = 省錢的選擇 (節省資源)
economic choice = 經濟學上的選擇 (系統層面)
🚫 錯誤選項 A 的原因：
「買便宜車」是「省錢的選擇」(economical)
不是「經濟系統的選擇」(economic)
第5題：文意推斷 ★★ 常考
The company found an _____ solution that reduced production costs by 30%.
(A) economical
(B) efficient
(C) economic
(D) effective
✓ 答案：A (economical)
關鍵詞彙：「reduced production costs by 30%」
→ 降低成本 → 節省資源
→ economical solution (節省成本的方案)
第6題：搭配應用 ★★
Her _____ lifestyle helps her save money .
(A) economic
(B) economical
(C) efficient
(D) thrifty
✓ 答案：B (economical)
economical lifestyle = 節省資源的生活方式
第7題：排除法 ★★
The _____ model of the new smartphone is popular among budget-conscious consumers .
(A) economical
(B) economic
(C) efficient
(D) effective
✓ 答案：A (economical)
「budget-conscious」= 重視預算 → economical
